## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, highly disciplined visual language for sovereign wealth custody, estate continuity, and generational asset vaulting. Drawing from Astral Antiquity, hermetic cartography, and ancient treasuries, the experience balances archaeological gravity with cryptographic finality.

### Personality & Tone
- **Cryptic Sovereignty:** Immovable, solemn, and deliberate. Every interaction feels archival, ceremonial, and permanent.
- **Astral Precision:** Mathematical structures, fine concentric calipers, astrolabe-inspired axis lines, and zero-radius structural framing.
- **Architectural Stillness:** Avoids frenetic micro-interactions and superficial trends. Interfaces behave like carved black granite and oxidized metal tablets.

### Target Persona & Emotional Impact
Ultra-high-net-worth lineages, institutional stewards, family offices, and sovereign trustees. The UI instills deep safety through cold permanence, quiet authority, and absolute unyielding structure rather than modern consumer friendliness.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is strictly mineral, archaic, and unyielding:

- **Neutral Prime (`#080909` - Mineral Obsidia):** The deep foundation across all screens. Layered tonal elevations use `#101111` (Basalt Shelf) and `#161717` (Altar Surface).
- **Secondary (`#D8D1C4` - Aged Parchment / Ivory):** The primary typographic ink and structural line color. Delivers timeless legibility with the soft warmth of centuries-old archival vellum.
- **Primary (`#B69567` - Oxidized Aurum):** Used for ritual points of focus, navigational coordinates, metric values, and primary action bounds.
- **Tertiary (`#8F282A` - Dried Carmine / Lacquer Seal):** Applied with deliberate restraint to mark irreversible mandates, cryptographic seals, security revocations, and critical ledger deltas.
- **Structural Stroke (`rgba(182, 149, 103, 0.2)`):** Fine double-rule divisions and astral crosshairs.

## Typography

The type system blends lapidary Roman proportion with the cadence of historical master printing:

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Roman monumental authority. High-contrast hairline serifs rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking convey cryptographic weight and solemn permanence.
- **Body (`EB Garamond`):** Bookish, scholarly, and frictionless to read across long-form covenants, custodial trust manifests, and audit transcripts.
- **Labels & Metrology (`Newsreader`):** Italic and small-caps treatments used for coordinates, ledger timestamps, vault identifiers, and astral calibration indices.
- **Transformations:** All section headers and key action buttons employ `text-transform: uppercase` paired with expanded letter spacing (`0.12em` to `0.22em`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an inflexible, ceremonial axial grid inspired by astronomical armillary spheres and classical treaties:

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column grid bound to a central vertical meridian axis. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer margins expanding to `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 8-column layout preserving centered dual-quadrant alignments.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column column structure. Astral decorative axes collapse into pure hairline horizontal thresholds (`1px` solid borders).

### Spacing Cadence
Distances between operational clusters honor classical mathematical proportions (1:1, 1:2, 1:3). Whitespace is deliberate and reverent, allowing large blocks of raw `#080909` to frame high-density ledger records.

## Elevation & Depth

No soft drop shadows, diffuse glows, or modern blur planes are permitted. Depth is achieved strictly through planar stacking and precision linecraft:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Dual Frames:** Surface boundaries are articulated through fine double borders—an outer hairline rule (`rgba(182, 149, 103, 0.25)`) separated by a `3px` void from an inner hairline rule (`rgba(216, 209, 196, 0.15)`).
- **Tonal Stepping:**
  - Base Plane: `#080909`
  - Inscription Plane (Vault / Card): `#101111`
  - Active / Inspect Plane: `#161717`
- **Astral Reticles:** Intersecting perpendicular rules create structural crosshairs at container corners (+ markings), emphasizing architectural rigidity.

## Shapes

The design system operates strictly at **zero roundedness (`rounded-none` / 0px)**. 

- Sharp, uncompromising corners across all primitives: buttons, modal overlays, drop-down tablets, input fields, and iconography.
- Micro-geometry is restricted to 45-degree chamfers on primary custodial seal buttons or quadrant badges, echoing stone-cut masonry and astrolabe index plates.

## Components

### Buttons & Mandate Triggers
- **Primary Execution Button:** Base background `#101111` with an outer border in `#B69567`, an inner offset frame (`2px`), and text in `#D8D1C4` (`label-md`, uppercase). Hover triggers an immediate inverted fill of `#B69567` with `#080909` text.
- **Carmine Protocol (Critical / Irrevocable):** Bordered in `#8F282A` with muted crimson fill (`rgba(143, 40, 42, 0.15)`). Used exclusively for asset movement, authorization signing, or cold-vault detachment.

### Cards & Ledger Monoliths
- Zero border radius. Encased within an oxidized double hairline boundary.
- Four corner intersection markers (`+`) positioned at `-4px` offsets in `#B69567`.
- Metric displays inside cards balance monumental numerals (`Bodoni Moda`) against dry, tracked metadata labels (`Newsreader`).

### Input Fields & Attestation Lines
- Minimalist open-bottom line or full framed black slate box (`#101111`).
- Bottom border: `1px solid rgba(216, 209, 196, 0.3)`. Focus state transforms border to `1px solid #B69567` accompanied by a micro Roman numeral coordinate indicator at the top right.

### Checkboxes & Binary Toggle Seals
- Square boxes (`14px x 14px`) with `0px` radius.
- Unchecked: `#080909` with a subtle `#B69567` outline.
- Checked: Contains an inscribed oxidized gold diamond (`◆`) or diagonal astrological cross (`✕`).

### Lists & Ledger Ledgers
- Alternating subtle base tones (`#080909` and `#0D0E0E`).
- Separated by hairline horizontal rules (`rgba(182, 149, 103, 0.15)`).
- Monospaced numerical values aligned flush-right; metadata tags set in `label-sm` small-caps.

### Specialized Component: The Custody Seal
- A bespoke ceremonial state badge for multi-signature assets. Composed of two concentric hairline rings framing the signatory progress, anchored in `#B69567` and finished with an oxidized carmine center point when sealed.
